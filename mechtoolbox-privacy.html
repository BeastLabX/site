MechToolbox Privacy Policy

This page explains how MechToolbox handles local app data, third-party services, exports, and user privacy.

Back to MechToolbox Terms

1. Information Collection

MechToolbox does not collect personal information such as your name, email address, or location.

2. Local Data Storage

All job data, fault entries, notes, manuals, saved references, and related workshop records are stored locally on your device unless you choose to export or share them.

3. User Files, Manuals, Photos, and Exports

MechToolbox may allow you to add or store job photos, fault photos, PDF manuals, backup files, and exported reports. These files are used only for app functionality on your device.

When you choose to share or export a report, PDF, image, manual, or backup file, that action is controlled by you through your device sharing options.

4. Third-Party Services

The app may use third-party services such as Google Play Billing, Google Play services, and Google AdMob. These services may process limited technical, purchase, advertising, diagnostics, or usage data according to their own privacy policies.

5. Advertising

MechToolbox may show ads in free or limited access areas of the app. Advertising services may use device or advertising identifiers to provide, measure, and improve ads.

6. Purchases and Subscriptions

MechToolbox may offer paid subscription options or unlock options through Google Play. Purchase and subscription handling is managed by Google Play. MechToolbox does not store your payment card details.

7. Data Security

Your app data remains on your device unless you choose to share it, export it, back it up, or restore it. You are responsible for keeping exported files and backup files secure after leaving the app.

8. No Professional Warranty

MechToolbox is designed as a workshop support tool. It does not replace professional judgement, manufacturer service information, legal compliance, or safety procedures.

9. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated in future versions. Updated versions will be posted on this website.

10. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, contact BeastLabX Pty Ltd at info@beastlabx.com.
