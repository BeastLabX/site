BeastUtility Privacy Policy

This page explains how BeastUtility handles gym calculator inputs, utility tool data, app preferences, exports, and user privacy.

Back to BeastUtility Terms

1. Information Collection

BeastUtility is built as a fast gym utility app. The app may store tool inputs, app preferences, recent values, calculator settings, and training utility data that you create or use inside the app.

BeastUtility does not require you to provide sensitive personal information directly to BeastLabX Pty Ltd to use the core app features.

2. Local App Data

Plate calculator values, warm-up calculator inputs, RPE or estimated 1RM values, set progression information, tool preferences, and similar app content may be stored locally on your device for app functionality.

If the app includes export, import, backup, sharing, or saved settings features, those actions are controlled by you through the app or your device sharing options.

3. Calculator and Training Tool Data

BeastUtility may use values entered by you to calculate plate loading, warm-up steps, RPE guidance, estimated maxes, set progression, and other gym utility outputs.

These values are used to display tool results inside the app. You are responsible for checking outputs before using them during training.

4. Third-Party Services

The app may use third-party services such as Google Play services, Google Play Billing, Google AdMob, crash reporting, analytics, or similar platform services. These services may process limited technical, diagnostics, purchase, advertising, or usage data according to their own privacy policies.

5. Advertising

BeastUtility may show ads in free or supported areas of the app. Advertising services may use device or advertising identifiers to provide, measure, and improve ads.

6. Purchases and Unlocks

BeastUtility may offer paid features, subscriptions, unlocks, or other purchase options through Google Play. Purchase and subscription handling is managed by Google Play. BeastUtility does not store your payment card details.

7. Shared and Exported Content

If you share screenshots, calculator results, saved settings, backups, or other files, that information may leave the app through your selected sharing method.

You are responsible for checking what you export or share and for keeping shared files secure after they leave the app.

8. Data Security

BeastUtility is designed to keep app-created utility data on your device unless you choose to export, share, back up, restore, or transfer it.

9. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated in future versions. Updated versions will be posted on this website.

10. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, contact BeastLabX Pty Ltd at info@beastlabx.com.
