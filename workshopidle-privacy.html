WorkshopIdle Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how WorkshopIdle handles information while the game is in development and after release.

Last updated: 8 May 2026

1. Overview

WorkshopIdle is a game developed by BeastLabX Pty Ltd. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, how it may be used, and what choices you have when using WorkshopIdle.

WorkshopIdle is currently in development. Features may change over time, and this policy may be updated as the game grows.

2. Information we do not directly collect

WorkshopIdle is planned as a simple game and does not currently require a user account. We do not directly ask for personal information such as your name, address, phone number, or identity document number to play the game.

3. Local game progress

WorkshopIdle may store game progress locally on your device. This may include information such as:

  • Game progress
  • Workshop upgrades
  • Cash, rewards, or in-game values
  • Settings or preferences
  • Daily reward or boost status

Local progress is used to keep your game state available on the same device. If you uninstall the game, clear app data, or change devices, local progress may be lost unless a backup or cloud feature is added later.

4. Advertising and rewarded ads

WorkshopIdle may include ads, including optional rewarded ads. Rewarded ads may allow players to receive in-game boosts or rewards after choosing to watch an ad.

Ads may be provided by third-party advertising services such as Google AdMob or similar providers. These services may collect information such as device identifiers, advertising identifiers, app interaction data, approximate location, diagnostics, and ad performance data, depending on your device settings and the provider's own policies.

5. Analytics, diagnostics, and crash information

WorkshopIdle may use app store, platform, or third-party tools to understand app performance, crashes, errors, and general usage. This helps improve stability and gameplay.

This information is generally technical and may include device type, operating system version, crash logs, session information, and similar diagnostic data.

6. Children

WorkshopIdle is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. If a parent or guardian believes that a child has provided personal information, they can contact us so we can review the request.

7. Third-party services

WorkshopIdle may use third-party services for advertising, app distribution, analytics, diagnostics, or platform features. These services operate under their own privacy policies and may process information according to their own terms.

8. How information is used

Information may be used to:

  • Run and improve WorkshopIdle
  • Save game progress on your device
  • Provide optional rewarded ads
  • Understand crashes and performance issues
  • Improve balancing, usability, and player experience
  • Comply with legal, platform, and app store requirements

9. Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by third-party services used to operate, distribute, advertise, or improve the game.

10. Your choices

You may be able to control certain privacy settings through your device settings, app permissions, advertising ID settings, or app store account settings. You can also uninstall the game at any time.

11. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as WorkshopIdle changes, new features are added, or legal and platform requirements change. The latest version will be made available on the BeastLabX website.

12. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or WorkshopIdle, contact BeastLabX Pty Ltd through the contact page on the BeastLabX website.
