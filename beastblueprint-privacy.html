BeastBlueprint Privacy Policy

This page explains how BeastBlueprint handles program builder data, generated plans, training preferences, exports to BeastLabX, and user privacy.

Back to BeastBlueprint Terms

1. Information Collection

BeastBlueprint is built as a structured program builder for training plans. The app may store program choices, goals, split selections, training level, focus areas, generated programs, exercise selections, edits, and app preferences that you create or use inside the app.

BeastBlueprint does not require you to provide sensitive personal information directly to BeastLabX Pty Ltd to use the core app features.

2. Local App Data

Program data, generated plans, selected exercises, training preferences, saved programs, edited workouts, and similar app content may be stored locally on your device for app functionality.

If the app includes export, import, backup, sharing, or transfer features, those actions are controlled by you through the app or your device sharing options.

3. BeastLabX Export and Import Flow

BeastBlueprint may allow training programs to be exported or sent into BeastLabX for workout tracking and execution.

Program data transferred from BeastBlueprint to BeastLabX is used to support training structure. You are responsible for reviewing programs before using them for training.

4. Training Preferences and Generated Programs

BeastBlueprint may use your selected goal, split, focus area, training level, block type, exercise choices, and program preferences to generate or adjust training plans.

This information is used to create and display your own training programs inside the app.

5. Third-Party Services

The app may use third-party services such as Google Play services, Google Play Billing, Google AdMob, crash reporting, analytics, or similar platform services. These services may process limited technical, diagnostics, purchase, advertising, or usage data according to their own privacy policies.

6. Advertising

BeastBlueprint may show ads in free or supported areas of the app. Advertising services may use device or advertising identifiers to provide, measure, and improve ads.

7. Purchases and Subscriptions

BeastBlueprint may offer paid features, subscriptions, unlocks, or other purchase options through Google Play. Purchase and subscription handling is managed by Google Play. BeastBlueprint does not store your payment card details.

8. Shared and Exported Content

If you export programs, share program files, send plans to BeastLabX, create backups, or share screenshots, that information may leave the app through your selected sharing method.

You are responsible for checking what you export or share and for keeping shared files secure after they leave the app.

9. Data Security

BeastBlueprint is designed to keep app-created program data on your device unless you choose to export, share, back up, restore, or transfer it.

10. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated in future versions. Updated versions will be posted on this website.

11. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, contact BeastLabX Pty Ltd at info@beastlabx.com.
